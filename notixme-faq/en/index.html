Frequently Asked Questions

This page answers the most common questions about using NotixMe.

Last updated

May 2026

What is NotixMe?

NotixMe is an app designed to help you create intentional moments throughout your day. It allows you to configure local reminders with custom phrases, time ranges, and weekly patterns.

Does NotixMe require internet?

No. NotixMe works completely offline. Your settings, phrases, history, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Does NotixMe collect my data?

No. NotixMe does not collect, store, or share personal data on external servers. The app does not use accounts, login, analytics, tracking, or advertising.

Why am I not receiving notifications?

This can happen for several reasons:

  • Notifications are not allowed for the app in system settings.
  • Do Not Disturb or Focus mode is enabled.
  • The configured time range has not started yet or has already ended.
  • The system may be restricting background activity or notifications.

Check your device notification settings to make sure NotixMe is allowed to send notifications.

How do I use the snooze option?

When available, you can snooze a reminder directly from the notification.

  • On iPhone: tap and hold the notification to view available actions. If NotixMe provides a snooze option for that reminder, it will appear among the quick actions.
  • On Android: expand the notification by tapping the arrow or opening its details. Some devices may also provide a built-in snooze option from the system.

The appearance of these options may vary depending on the device, OS version, and notification settings.

What are persistent notifications?

Persistent notifications are notifications that do not disappear automatically after a few seconds. Instead, they remain visible on the lock screen or notification center until you interact with them.

In NotixMe, this behavior can help keep reminders visible for longer, so you can come back to them when you're ready.

  • On iPhone: this behavior depends on system notification settings, including banners, lock screen, notification center, and Focus modes.
  • On Android: the behavior may vary depending on the Android version, device manufacturer, and app notification settings.

If notifications are disappearing too quickly, check your device’s notification settings for NotixMe.

How do reminders work?

You define a time range, the number of reminders, and the days of the week you want them. NotixMe schedules local notifications based on these settings.

What is the difference between distributed and random reminders?

In distributed mode, notifications are spaced evenly within the selected time range. In random mode, the app chooses different times within the configured period.

If the time range is too short for the number of reminders, the app may automatically adjust the distribution to avoid notifications being too close together.

Can I create my own phrases?

Yes. You can create, edit, and delete your own custom phrases inside the app.

Is my history saved?

Yes. Your history is stored locally on your device so you can track your reminders and interactions.

Since data is stored locally, deleting the app will remove all saved information.

Does NotixMe consume a lot of battery?

No. NotixMe uses local system notifications and does not rely on heavy background processes.

Can I use NotixMe on multiple devices?

NotixMe does not sync data across devices. Since everything is stored locally, each device has its own settings, phrases, and history.

How do I change the app language?

NotixMe follows your device language settings when available.
