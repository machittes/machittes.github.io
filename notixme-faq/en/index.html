When available, you can snooze a reminder directly from the notification.

  • On iPhone: tap and hold the notification to view available actions. If NotixMe provides a snooze option for that reminder, it will appear among the quick actions.
  • On Android: expand the notification by tapping the arrow or opening its details. Some devices may also provide a built-in snooze option from the system.

The appearance of these options may vary depending on the device, OS version, and notification settings.

Persistent notifications are notifications that do not disappear automatically after a few seconds. Instead, they remain visible on the lock screen or notification center until you interact with them.

In NotixMe, this behavior can help keep reminders visible for longer, so you can come back to them when you're ready.

  • On iPhone: this behavior depends on system notification settings, including banners, lock screen, notification center, and Focus modes.
  • On Android: the behavior may vary depending on the Android version, device manufacturer, and app notification settings.

If notifications are disappearing too quickly, check your device's notification settings for NotixMe.

You define a time range, the number of reminders, and the days of the week you want them. NotixMe schedules local notifications based on these settings.

In distributed mode, notifications are spaced evenly within the selected time range. In random mode, the app chooses different times within the configured period.

If the time range is too short for the number of reminders, the app may automatically adjust the distribution to avoid notifications being too close together.
