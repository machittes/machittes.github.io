Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NotixMe handles information when you use the app.

Last updated

March 2026

Overview

NotixMe is an offline-first app designed to help you create intentional moments throughout your day. The app is built to work without accounts, analytics, or external servers.

Information Collection

NotixMe does not collect, store, or share personal data on external servers. All content you create in the app (such as your titles, phrases, categories, history, and preferences) is stored locally on your device.

Deleting the app removes locally stored information from the device.

Permissions

NotixMe may request permission to deliver local, on-device signals at the times you define. This permission is used only to enable the core functionality you configure in the app.

Analytics and Tracking

NotixMe does not use analytics tools, tracking technologies, advertising identifiers, or third-party SDKs to profile users or collect behavioral data.

Third-Party Services

NotixMe does not integrate with third-party services that collect user data. The app does not require sign-in and does not send your content to any server.

Data Security

Since information is stored locally on your device, protecting access to your device helps protect your data. If you share your device or your device is compromised, others may be able to access locally stored content.

Children’s Privacy

NotixMe does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Because the app does not use accounts or external servers, it does not build user profiles.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
